What is this?

This tool tokenizes text exactly as GPT-4 does, using the cl100k_base vocabulary of ~100,000 tokens. It runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent anywhere.

Tokenization is how language models read text: they don't see characters or words, they see tokens — chunks determined by a Byte Pair Encoding (BPE) algorithm trained on a large corpus of text.
